    About

    RateMyApartment is built and maintained by ASUCD’s Innovation and Research Lab (IRL).

    It started as a way for students to explore the results of our annual housing survey more intuitively.

    As it grows, UC Davis students will be able to sign in and report their satisfaction with their rent, location, amenities, and communication with their landlord.

    How ratings are calculated

    Every score is out of 5. Survey answers from “Very dissatisfied” (1) to “Very satisfied” (5) are grouped into four categories:

    Maintenance
    Satisfaction with maintenance and property management.
    Location
    Access to the nearest grocery store, bus stop, entertainment, and campus.
    Livability
    Quietness, safety, and space or privacy.
    Value
    “Was your housing worth the cost?” Yes counts as 5 and No as 1, so 3.0 means an even split.

    The overall score is the average of the four. Properties with fewer than 5 responses are marked limited data.
